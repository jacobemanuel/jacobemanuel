# Hi, I'm Jakub 👋

I build spatial software for Apple Vision Pro, and I don't believe a feature works until I've seen it on the headset. Most of what I do sits where 3D content, rendering and real hardware meet: getting heavy 3D data onto a headset, making it behave in your hands, and measuring what it actually costs the device.

**What I'm up to**

- XR engineer on an automotive Apple Vision Pro app: full-scale vehicle reviews, rendered on the device;
- researching Gaussian Splatting on Apple Vision Pro at TUM;
- building XR apps for clients as an independent developer;
- Head of Strategic Development at TUM-XR, the student XR initiative at TUM, where I secured €5,000 in seed funding for a Gaussian Splatting initiative, and Senior Associate at [XR Association Munich e.V.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/xrmunich/)

**Before that:** two years of industrial XR. I helped move VR training from streaming to on-device and demoed it at ACHEMA 2024, including to Saudi Arabia's Deputy Minister for Industry Affairs.

**Studying:** M.Sc. Management & Technology at TUM, with Computer Engineering as my technology specialization.
